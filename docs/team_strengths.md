## 3. Work allocation principles
- Assign ownership using demonstrated skills, interest, availability, and project dependencies.
- Give each subsystem one accountable owner and at least one reviewer/support member.
- Avoid assigning all critical knowledge to one person; pair members and document interfaces.
- Revisit the allocation after the literature survey and after the design freeze.

## 4. Workload / strength estimates

Any percentage estimates supplied by the team must have a clearly defined meaning (for example, relative proficiency, available effort, or expected workload). They are not interchangeable. Confirm the meaning and ensure workload percentages sum to 100% before using them for task allocation.
